FAQ

Is this makeup?这算化妆吗？

It's the same stuff news anchors and actors use every day: a concealer stick and blotting sheets. Used right, nobody can tell. That's the whole point.用的就是主播和演员天天用的东西：遮瑕棒和吸油纸。用对了没人看得出来，这就是重点。

Is it a filter?是滤镜吗？

No. The preview shows you the target; then you do it for real. Shots of the preview are marked "Preview". Shots taken with nothing drawn are marked "Real. No filter."不是。预览只是让你看目标，然后你真的去做。预览截图会标「Preview」，什么都没画的真实照片才标「Real. No filter.」。

How do I install the APK?APK 怎么安装？
  1. Tap "Download for Android" on your phone.在手机上点「下载安卓版」。
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.打开下载的文件。如果系统提示，允许浏览器安装应用。
  3. Tap Install. Updates come inside the app after that.点「安装」。之后的更新在 App 里就能完成。
Why 18+?为什么要 18 岁以上？

Face data rules for under-18s are strict, and we'd rather get it right than rush it.未成年人的人脸数据规定很严，我们宁可做对，不抢时间。

iPhone?有 iPhone 版吗？

Not yet. Android first.还没有，先做安卓版。
